Who We Are

A Reliable Partner for International Trade & Freight

Founded on the values of transparency, speed, and uncompromising integrity, Savelanded Import and Export Company Limited was created to eliminate the traditional risks, hidden charges, and communication barriers that Nigerian entrepreneurs face when dealing with Chinese suppliers. In high-volume trade hubs from Lagos to Kano, businesses rely on predictable supply chains governed by the World Trade Organization (WTO) standards.

We are not merely a freight-forwarding agency. We are an end-to-end international trade enabler. We support our clients across the entire import lifecycle: from physical on-ground factory vetting and product sourcing in Yiwu and Guangzhou, to RMB procurement payments, cargo consolidation, customs clearance, and secure door-to-door delivery in Lagos and all 36 Nigerian states.

5-Stage Anti-Fraud & Verification Protocol

Importers frequently lose funds to unverified online storefronts, counterfeit items, or shipping middlemen who disappear. Savelanded shields your capital through an established 5-stage verification standard:

  • ✓
    Chinese Legal Entity Check: We cross-reference the manufacturer's Unified Social Credit Code with the Chinese State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR).
  • ✓
    Physical Factory & Stall Audit: Our representatives physically visit the supplier’s booth in Yiwu International Trade City or manufacturing plant in Guangdong.
  • ✓
    Pre-Production Sample Validation: We inspect physical sample units, conduct stress testing, and take HD video proof before authorizing bulk runs.
  • ✓
    Milestone Payment Custody: We release payments in tranches (typically 30% initial deposit, 70% only upon verified warehouse delivery in China).
  • ✓
    Carton Inspection & Reinforcement: Fragile cargo is packaged with waterproof wrapping, corner guards, and wooden crating prior to container stuffing.
