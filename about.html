Who We Are

A Reliable Partner for International Trade & Freight

Founded on the values of transparency, speed, and uncompromising integrity, Savelanded Import and Export Company Limited was created to eliminate the traditional risks, hidden charges, and communication barriers that Nigerian entrepreneurs face when dealing with Chinese suppliers.

We are not merely a freight-forwarding agency. We are an end-to-end international trade enabler. We support our clients across the entire import lifecycle: from physical on-ground factory vetting and product sourcing in Yiwu and Guangzhou, to RMB procurement payments, cargo consolidation, customs clearance, and secure door-to-door delivery in Lagos and all 36 Nigerian states.

Our Core Values

1. Total Transparency
No hidden tariffs, surprise clearance charges, or inflated exchange rates. Every invoice and cargo rate is detailed upfront.
2. On-Ground Presence
With operational offices and consolidation warehouses in Yiwu and Guangzhou, our team physically verifies cargo before shipping.
3. Strict Quality Control
We perform carton inspection, piece counts, and functional testing to protect your capital from counterfeit or damaged products.
4. Cargo Accountability
From factory gate in China to warehouse handover at Trade Fair Complex, Lagos, your goods are secured with barcode tracking.

5-Stage Anti-Fraud & Verification Protocol

Importers frequently lose funds to unverified online storefronts, counterfeit items, or shipping middlemen who disappear. Savelanded shields your capital through an established 5-stage verification standard:

  • ✓
    Chinese Corporate Registration Audit: We cross-reference supplier business licenses on the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.
  • ✓
    Physical Factory & Market Inspection: Our on-ground representatives visit production lines in Guangdong and Zhejiang to verify physical operations.
  • ✓
    Sample Acquisition & Pre-Production Approval: We obtain and examine production samples, sending high-resolution media reports to your Lagos desk.
  • ✓
    Controlled RMB/Yuan Disbursement: Funds are disbursed under milestone contracts, preventing supplier default or production abandonment.
  • ✓
    Consolidation Warehouse Audit: Goods arriving at our Yiwu and Guangzhou hubs undergo carton integrity and packaging reinforcement before loading.
