Shipping Comparison

Choose the Shipping Option That Works for You.

Understand the key attributes of air cargo and sea freight to make informed decisions for your business.

Air Freight

Priority Transit • Fast Turnaround

Suitable for customers prioritising speed and urgent supply chain cycles.

  • Suitability: Useful for suitable smaller or time-sensitive cargo.
  • Key Focus: Rapid turnaround for electronics, samples, and high-demand commercial inventory.
  • Conditions: Subject to cargo restrictions and applicable air transport charges.
  • Rate Dynamic: Actual cost and timing depend on cargo weight, volume, and airline departures.

Sea Freight

Maximum Economy • Bulk Volume

Suitable for many bulk and larger shipments prioritizing cargo volume and shipping economics.

  • Suitability: An option for customers prioritizing cargo volume, container loads, and shipping economics.
  • Cargo Types: Industrial equipment, heavy merchandise, raw materials, furniture, and wholesale goods.
  • Conditions: Subject to ocean vessel schedules and applicable maritime port charges.
  • Rate Dynamic: Rates depend on container type (FCL/LCL), CBM volume, and destination requirements.
