Sea Freight from China to Nigeria Overview

Overview

High-Capacity, Economical Ocean Freight

When shipping large volumes, heavy commodities, or planning bulk stock replenishment, sea freight provides unmatched economic efficiency. Savelanded coordinates container bookings with major global ocean shipping lines adhering to International Maritime Organization (IMO) maritime conventions, loading from key Chinese coastal ports:

  • ✓
    Ningbo-Zhoushan & Shanghai: Serving Zhejiang, Jiangsu, and Yiwu manufacturers.
  • ✓
    Guangzhou (Nansha) & Shenzhen (Yantian/Shekou): Serving the Guangdong industrial heartland.

Whether your destination is Lagos Apapa Port, Tin Can Island, or delivery straight to your warehouse, our team coordinates the entire marine voyage. For guidance on customs compliance, see our customs documentation and PAAR service.

FCL vs. LCL: Which Option Fits Your Shipment?

Feature Full Container Load (FCL) Groupage Cargo (LCL)
Container Sizes 20ft General Purpose, 40ft High Cube (HC) Consolidated in shared container
Billing Basis Flat rate per container Billed per Cubic Meter (CBM)
Transit Time 28 – 35 Days Direct Voyage 32 – 40 Days (including consolidation)
Minimum Volume 1 Full Container (approx. 28 CBM+) From 0.5 CBM upwards
Customs Handling Direct terminal clearing under single consignee Consolidated clearing under master bill
Best Suited For Heavy machinery, bulk raw materials, tiles Small to medium retailers, sample batches

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions about our operations and services.

What is the cost structure of sea shipping from China to Lagos?

Sea freight is billed either per Cubic Meter (CBM) for LCL groupage cargo or as a flat container rate for FCL (20ft or 40ft High Cube containers). Pricing includes ocean vessel carriage, marine documentation, terminal handling, and Lagos customs clearance options.

How long does ocean container shipping take from China to Lagos ports?

Ocean transit takes approximately 28 to 38 days on water departing from Ningbo-Zhoushan, Shanghai, or Guangzhou (Nansha) to Lagos ports (Apapa Port or Tin Can Island), followed by 5 to 7 days for customs inspection and terminal offloading.

Can I ship less than a full container (LCL) from China?

Yes. Savelanded provides Less than Container Load (LCL Groupage) starting from 0.5 CBM upwards. We combine your cartons into shared containers with other importers, saving you the expense of chartering an entire container.
