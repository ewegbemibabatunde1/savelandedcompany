20ft vs. 40ft Containers & Accurate CBM Calculations

Cubic Meters (CBM) measure cargo volume. In ocean shipping, rates are quoted per CBM. Knowing standard maritime equipment capacities allows you to negotiate factory carton packing sizes to maximize container payload.

Maritime CBM Calculation Formula

CBM per carton = [Length (m) × Width (m) × Height (m)]
Example: A carton measuring 50cm × 40cm × 40cm = 0.50m × 0.40m × 0.40m = 0.08 CBM. If you have 50 cartons: 50 × 0.08 = 4.0 CBM total volume.

Chinese Loading Ports & Nigerian Destination Terminals

Vessels sail from premier deepwater container hubs in China following international maritime conventions supervised by the International Maritime Organization (IMO):

  • •

Ocean Transit Times & Key Cost Factors

Port-to-port ocean transit from China to Lagos typically ranges between 35 to 45 days depending on whether the vessel sails directly through the Cape of Good Hope or transships at international ports such as Singapore or Pointe-Noire.

Key cost components include ocean freight (O/F), bunker adjustment factors (BAF), currency adjustment factors (CAF), terminal handling charges (THC) at both load and discharge ports, Nigerian port tariffs, and demurrage avoidance strategies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions about our operations and services.

What is the cost structure of sea shipping from China to Lagos?

Sea freight is billed either per Cubic Meter (CBM) for LCL groupage cargo or as a flat container rate for FCL (20ft or 40ft High Cube containers). Pricing includes ocean vessel carriage, marine documentation, terminal handling, and Lagos customs clearance options.

How long does ocean container shipping take from China to Lagos ports?

Ocean transit takes approximately 28 to 38 days on water departing from Ningbo-Zhoushan, Shanghai, or Guangzhou (Nansha) to Lagos ports (Apapa Port or Tin Can Island), followed by 5 to 7 days for customs inspection and terminal offloading.

Can I ship less than a full container (LCL) from China?

Yes. Savelanded provides Less than Container Load (LCL Groupage) starting from 0.5 CBM upwards. We combine your cartons into shared containers with other importers, saving you the expense of chartering an entire container.
