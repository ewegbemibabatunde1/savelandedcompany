Overview

Streamlined, Risk-Free Cross-Border Procurement

One of the greatest challenges facing Nigerian importers is international payment settlement. Daily card limits, bank Form A backlogs, fluctuating foreign exchange rates, and strict supplier terms often bring business operations to a halt. Trade transactions conform to monetary guidance published by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN).

Savelanded provides a reliable, transparent bridge. By operating verified commercial bank accounts and corporate payment gateways in China, we convert your capital safely, paying suppliers in their local currency (RMB / Chinese Yuan) or USD within hours of order confirmation. Once your order is ready, we seamlessly arrange sea container transport or express air cargo.

Solving the Nigerian Foreign Exchange Barrier

Direct RMB / Yuan Transfers
Chinese domestic suppliers prefer payment in RMB via bank transfer, WeChat Pay, or Alipay. We make direct RMB disbursements with instant transfer receipts.
Competitive Market Exchange Rates
Clear, transparent conversion rates without hidden administrative fees or unannounced bank deductions.
Commercial Contract Protection
We review purchase invoices (PI) and manufacturing agreements to protect you against unexpected price hikes or substandard goods.
Tax & Export Rebate Guidance
For major container-level purchases, we advise on Chinese export tax rebates and compliant customs invoice declaration.

Milestone Payment Custody: Protecting Your Capital

Sending 100% of your funds to an overseas factory before production is completed is a severe financial risk. Savelanded safeguards your money with our 30/70 milestone escrow mechanism:

  • 1
    30% Production Deposit: Released to the factory only after business identity verification and purchase contract confirmation.
  • 2
    Production Monitoring: Star and our China operations team monitor factory output and milestone deadlines.
  • 3
    Final Quality Inspection: When goods arrive at our Yiwu or Guangzhou warehouse, cartons are opened, counted, and inspected.
  • 4
    70% Balance Release: Only after the customer verifies quality photos or video reports is the supplier paid in full.

End-to-End Purchase Order Tracking

You receive detailed tracking from the moment a deposit is made until packages arrive at our warehouse. We maintain direct communication with factory managers to ensure that production deadlines, custom branding, and shipping mark requirements are fulfilled accurately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions about our operations and services.

How do I pay Chinese factories in Chinese Yuan (RMB) from Nigeria?

Nigerian clients pay in Nigerian Naira (NGN) into Savelanded's corporate bank accounts. Savelanded manages currency conversion and pays suppliers directly in Chinese Yuan (RMB via bank transfer, Alipay, or WeChat Pay) or USD within 2 to 4 business hours, providing official bank transfer vouchers.

How does Savelanded's 30/70 milestone escrow protect my funds?

Instead of paying 100% upfront to an overseas supplier, Savelanded disburses a 30% initial deposit to initiate production. The remaining 70% balance is released only after our inspectors in Yiwu or Guangzhou examine the completed order and verify quality.

Can Savelanded help buy directly from 1688 sellers who don't speak English?

Yes. Our native bilingual operations team in Yiwu manages the entire communication, order placement, discount negotiations, and delivery tracking on 1688.com.
