Savelanded China–Nigeria Logistics & Trade Services

Savelanded Import and Export Company Limited provides end-to-end commercial trade execution between China and Nigeria. Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions about our operations and services.

What services does Savelanded provide for China to Nigeria trade?

Savelanded provides 8 core services: 1) Air freight forwarding from Guangzhou to Lagos, 2) Sea freight FCL and LCL groupage per CBM, 3) Product sourcing in Yiwu Futian and 1688, 4) Purchasing in Chinese Yuan (RMB) with escrow protection, 5) Warehousing and multi-supplier package consolidation, 6) Customs clearance and Form M/PAAR processing, 7) International import/export compliance, and 8) All-inclusive door-to-door delivery across all 36 Nigerian states.

How fast is air cargo compared to sea freight from China to Nigeria?

Air cargo takes 5 to 8 working days from Guangzhou (CAN) to Lagos MMIA Airport clearing, billed per kg. Sea freight takes 28 to 38 days on ocean water from Ningbo or Guangzhou to Lagos ports (Apapa & Tin Can), billed per CBM or full container.

How do I request a shipping or sourcing quote from Savelanded?

You can submit cargo weight, carton dimensions, or supplier product links via our online Quote Wizard on quote.html, or contact CEO Wolex (+86 182 4854 7224) or Operations Lead Star (+86 157 5799 1458) on WhatsApp for an immediate quotation.
