How Does Shipping from China to Nigeria Work?

Shipping cargo from China to Nigeria is carried out via two primary modes: Air Freight (5 to 8 working days, charged per kg on the greater of scale weight vs. volumetric weight L×W×H÷6,000) for urgent, high-value, or lightweight commercial consignments; and Sea Freight (35 to 45 sailing days, charged per Cubic Meter (CBM) for LCL groupage or flat rates for 20ft/40ft FCL containers) for heavy machinery, construction materials, and bulk wholesale inventory. Savelanded receives your parcels at our Guangzhou and Yiwu warehouses, consolidates orders from 1688 and Alibaba suppliers, oversees China export documentation, executes customs clearance at Lagos ports, and delivers to our Trade Fair Complex hub or nationwide doorstep.

1. Shipping Services & Transport Modes from China to Nigeria

Whether you are an established wholesaler importing heavy industrial machinery or an e-commerce vendor moving fast-fashion apparel from 1688, selecting the optimal freight mode is crucial for protecting your margins and maintaining reliable inventory turnover. Savelanded operates five specialized shipping solutions across the China–Nigeria trade corridor:

A. Scheduled Commercial Air Cargo (Standard & Sensitive)

Operating out of Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN) and Shenzhen Bao'an (SZX) with direct connections into Murtala Muhammed International Airport (LOS) in Lagos, our scheduled air cargo is engineered for velocity. Flights depart multiple times each week. We differentiate between:

  • General Goods: Clothing, shoes, textiles, non-branded consumer goods, auto spares, and hardware.
  • Sensitive & Battery Cargo: Smartphones, laptops, lithium-ion power banks, solar batteries, cosmetic liquids, lotions, and powders requiring dedicated dangerous goods (DG) documentation and specialized cargo flights.

Learn more on our dedicated Air Freight from China to Nigeria service page.

B. Ocean Freight LCL (Less than Container Load / Groupage)

For cargo volumes ranging from 0.1 CBM to 15 CBM, LCL groupage is the most economical shipping method available. Instead of paying for an entire 20ft container, your cartons share container space with shipments from other verified Nigerian importers. Savelanded securely consolidates, packs, pallets, and shrink-wraps your cargo at our Guangzhou and Yiwu facilities. Vessels sail weekly from Ningbo-Zhoushan, Guangzhou (Nansha), and Shenzhen (Shekou) into Lagos Apapa and Tin Can Island ports.

C. Ocean Freight FCL (Full Container Load: 20ft & 40ft HQ)

When shipping high-volume merchandise, raw industrial materials, ceramic tiles, or heavy machinery, booking a dedicated 20ft GP (General Purpose, ~28 CBM capacity) or 40ft HQ (High Cube, ~68 CBM capacity) container provides maximum per-unit cost efficiency. We manage direct factory pick-up across China, export custom declarations, ocean carrier booking (with major lines like Maersk, MSC, COSCO, and CMA CGM), and terminal handling at Apapa, Tin Can, or the modern Lekki Deep Sea Port.

Read full technical container dimensions on our Sea Freight China to Nigeria page.

D. Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) All-Inclusive Door-to-Door Delivery

Our DDP service eliminates all customs and logistics friction for Nigerian entrepreneurs. We handle the entire lifecycle: receiving goods at our China warehouse, international freight, clearing duties through Nigeria Customs Service, and delivering safely to our Trade Fair Complex office in Lagos or directly to your business address nationwide. No surprise demurrage, no separate terminal clearance invoices, and no customs queries.

Explore how this works on our Door-to-Door Delivery Service page.

E. Express Air Courier (Emergency Spares & Commercial Samples)

When product samples, urgent prototypes, legal commercial contracts, or critical factory spare parts are needed within 3 to 5 business days, our express courier pipeline delivers rapid airport-to-door delivery with live digital checkpoint tracking.

7. Nigerian Customs Clearance, Form M, PAAR & SONCAP

Clearing cargo through Nigerian ports (Apapa, Tin Can Island, Lekki Port, or Lagos Airport) requires rigorous adherence to statutory trade regulations managed by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), Nigeria Customs Service (NCS), and regulatory bodies like the Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON) and NAFDAC.

Statutory Documentation for Formal Container Imports (FCL)

  • e-Form M: The statutory import declaration registered through an Authorized Dealer Bank prior to cargo departure in China to record foreign exchange allocation.
  • Pre-Arrival Assessment Report (PAAR): Issued electronically by Nigeria Customs Service to determine exact tariff valuation and payable customs duty based on the Harmonized System (HS) code.
  • SONCAP Certificate (Standards Organisation of Nigeria): Mandatory for regulated manufactured products, electrical appliances, automotive spares, and building materials to verify safety and standards. Requires a preliminary Product Certificate (PC) before shipment.
  • NAFDAC Import Permits: Compulsory for packaged foods, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, and specialty chemicals.
  • Commercial Shipping Documents: Final Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, and Marine Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB).
Skip the Regulatory Headache with Savelanded DDP Groupage:
If you do not possess a registered corporate Form M or wish to avoid complex documentation queries, our Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) Groupage service handles all customs clearance under our accredited master trade manifests. You pay one transparent all-inclusive rate per kg or per CBM, and pick up your cargo cleared and duty-paid at our Trade Fair hub. Visit our Customs Clearance & Documentation page for details.

12. Frequently Asked Questions (Shipping from China to Nigeria)

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions regarding rates, customs clearance, transit times, and cargo consolidation.

How much does it cost to ship 1kg of cargo from China to Nigeria?

Air freight rates from China (Guangzhou/Shenzhen) to Lagos, Nigeria typically range between $8.00 and $13.00 per kilogram for standard commercial cargo, depending on overall consignment weight and current airline fuel surcharges. Sensitive goods such as electronics with lithium batteries or liquids usually carry a higher surcharge ($10.50–$14.50/kg). Always contact our logistics desk for real-time live quotes.

What is the difference between actual scale weight and volumetric weight for air freight?

Actual weight is the physical weight of your carton on a calibrated scale in kilograms. Volumetric weight measures the space the carton occupies inside the aircraft, calculated as (Length × Width × Height in cm) ÷ 6,000. Airlines charge based on whichever number is higher (chargeable weight). For example, a 7kg box measuring 60×40×40cm has a volumetric weight of 16kg, so you will be billed on 16kg.

How is CBM calculated for sea freight, and what is the minimum volume?

Cubic Meter (CBM) is calculated using the formula: (Length × Width × Height in cm) ÷ 1,000,000 multiplied by the number of cartons. Savelanded accepts sea freight LCL (groupage) shipments starting from 0.1 CBM, allowing small-scale and growing importers to access economical ocean rates without needing to fill an entire 20ft or 40ft container.

How long does shipping take from China to Nigeria by air and by sea?

Air freight typically arrives in Lagos (Murtala Muhammed International Airport) within 5 to 8 working days from flight departure in Guangzhou or Shenzhen. Sea freight from major Chinese ports (Ningbo, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Qingdao) to Lagos ports (Apapa, Tin Can Island, Lekki Deep Sea Port) requires approximately 35 to 45 sailing and terminal processing days.

How can I purchase goods on 1688, Taobao, or Alibaba and ship them with Savelanded?

You can source items directly on 1688, Taobao, or Alibaba and instruct your suppliers to ship them to our Guangzhou or Yiwu warehouse marked with your unique Savelanded Client ID. Alternatively, you can use our procurement service: provide us with the product links, pay us in Nigerian Naira (NGN) or USD, and our China team will pay the Chinese suppliers directly in Chinese Yuan (RMB/CNY) to avoid parallel FX difficulties.

Can I ship products containing lithium batteries or liquids to Nigeria?

Yes. Savelanded operates dedicated sensitive cargo channels for items containing built-in lithium-ion batteries (smartphones, power banks, solar lights, laptops) as well as approved cosmetic liquids and pastes. These goods require specialized airline hazard declarations and are booked on designated cargo freighters.

What documents are required to clear commercial containers in Nigeria?

For formal container shipping (FCL), statutory requirements enforced by Nigeria Customs Service include the electronic Form M, Pre-Arrival Assessment Report (PAAR), Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and SONCAP Certificate (or NAFDAC permit for food/chemicals/cosmetics). If you use Savelanded's DDP all-inclusive door-to-door groupage service, we handle all clearing and terminal formalities on your behalf.

Does Savelanded deliver outside Lagos to Abuja, Port Harcourt, or Kano?

Yes. While our primary Nigerian consolidation depot is located at the Trade Fair Complex in Lagos, we provide seamless interstate on-forwarding to all 36 Nigerian states, including dedicated door delivery routes to Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, Onitsha, Aba, and Ibadan.

What payment methods and currencies are accepted for freight and procurement?

Savelanded accepts payments in Nigerian Naira (NGN), US Dollars (USD), and Chinese Yuan (RMB/CNY). In accordance with company policy, 100% of the freight invoice must be settled prior to cargo departure from China.

Is transit cargo insurance included or available as an optional add-on?

Standard carriage includes baseline carrier liability. However, for high-value merchandise, machinery, or fragile electronics, Savelanded offers comprehensive transit cargo insurance as an optional add-on (typically 1% to 2% of invoice value), providing full indemnity against total loss or maritime damage.
