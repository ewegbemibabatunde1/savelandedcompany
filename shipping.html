Worked Pricing Examples (Using Verified Rates)

Worked Example 1: Air Freight Volumetric Calculation

An importer purchases 5 cartons of branded sneakers from Guangzhou. Each carton measures 60cm × 40cm × 40cm and weighs 12 kg on the scale.

  • Total Physical Scale Weight: 5 cartons × 12 kg = 60 kg.
  • Volumetric Weight per carton: (60 × 40 × 40) ÷ 6,000 = 16 kg.
  • Total Volumetric Weight: 5 cartons × 16 kg = 80 kg.
  • Chargeable Weight Applied: Because the volumetric weight (80 kg) is greater than the scale weight (60 kg), airlines bill on 80 kg.
  • Estimated Air Freight: 80 kg falls into the 46–100 kg commercial tier ($8.50–$10.00/kg) = approximately $680 – $800 total freight, delivering to Lagos in 5–8 working days.

Worked Example 2: Sea Freight LCL (Groupage) CBM Calculation

A merchant orders 20 cartons of kitchen blenders from Yiwu. Each carton measures 50cm × 40cm × 40cm and weighs 18 kg (total gross weight = 360 kg).

  • Volume per carton in CBM: (50 × 40 × 40) ÷ 1,000,000 = 0.08 CBM.
  • Total Consignment Volume: 20 cartons × 0.08 CBM = 1.60 CBM.
  • Air Freight Comparison: 360 kg via air would cost over $2,800.
  • Sea Freight LCL Cost: 1.60 CBM billed at the indicative rate of $180–$280/CBM = approximately $288 – $448 total ocean freight!
  • Conclusion: For non-urgent commercial merchandise, sea freight LCL saves the merchant over 80% in transport expenditure.

4. Sea Freight from China to Nigeria (LCL, FCL & CBM Calculation)

Maritime ocean shipping is the economic backbone of Nigeria's wholesale and manufacturing sectors. When cargo weight exceeds 200–300kg or volume exceeds 1–2 CBM, sea freight delivers massive cost savings over air freight. Savelanded coordinates regular ocean sailings from Ningbo-Zhoushan, Guangzhou (Nansha), Shenzhen (Shekou/Yantian), Shanghai, and Qingdao into Lagos seaports: Apapa Port, Tin Can Island, and Lekki Deep Sea Port.

LCL (Less than Container Load / Groupage)

If your cargo does not fill an entire 20ft container, LCL groupage allows you to share container space with other verified Nigerian merchants. You pay strictly for the Cubic Meters (CBM) your cartons occupy. Savelanded securely palletizes, wraps, and loads your boxes into shared master containers, providing small-and-medium businesses with wholesale container shipping rates without large capital lock-up.

FCL (Full Container Load: 20ft & 40ft Containers)

For large industrial factories, wholesale distributors, and commodity importers, full container load shipping provides exclusive container usage, zero co-loading risks, and maximum per-unit economy:

  • 20ft Standard GP Container: Holds approximately 28 to 30 CBM of cargo with a payload capacity up to 25–28 tonnes. Ideal for heavy, dense cargo such as ceramic tiles, steel bolts, chemicals, or automotive spares.
  • 40ft High Cube (40HQ) Container: Holds approximately 68 to 72 CBM with a maximum payload around 26–28 tonnes. Perfect for high-volume, lower-density commodities such as furniture, plastics, toys, textiles, and packaged consumer goods.

9. Nigerian Customs and Import Requirements

Commercial imports into Nigeria must comply with regulatory standards established by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), Nigeria Customs Service (NCS), Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON), and NAFDAC. Requirements vary based on shipment type:

Statutory Documentation for Formal Container Imports (FCL)

  • Commercial Invoice & Packing List: Itemized details of goods, unit values, HS codes, and carton breakdown issued by the Chinese vendor.
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB): Official contract of carriage issued by the shipping line or airline carrier.
  • e-Form M: Mandatory import application submitted through an Authorized Dealer Bank via the Nigeria Single Window for Trade before goods are shipped.
  • Pre-Arrival Assessment Report (PAAR): Issued electronically by Nigeria Customs Service calculating payable import duties and statutory taxes.
  • SONCAP Certificate (SON): Mandatory conformity assessment verifying product safety for manufactured items, electricals, and building supplies. Requires an initial Product Certificate (PC).
  • NAFDAC Permits: Compulsory for packaged food, medical products, cosmetics, and specialty chemicals.
How Savelanded DDP Groupage Eliminates Documentation Friction:
If you are importing via our Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) Groupage service, Savelanded clears your cargo under our corporate master trade manifest. You do not need to open an individual Form M or obtain separate PAAR documentation. We manage customs tariffs, inspections, and port clearances on your behalf, allowing you to focus on your core business.

12. Frequently Asked Questions (Shipping from China to Nigeria)

Direct, transparent answers to the 18 most critical questions about shipping rates, methods, customs clearance, and delivery across Nigeria.

1. How much does it cost to ship from China to Nigeria?

Shipping costs depend on the shipping method, weight, volume, and cargo nature. Air freight generally ranges between $7.80 and $13.50 per kg depending on total weight tier (higher volume qualifies for lower per-kg rates), plus local clearance. Sea freight LCL (groupage) is billed per Cubic Meter (CBM), typically starting from $180 to $280 per CBM for general merchandise, with full 20ft and 40ft container ocean baselines ranging from $2,800 to $4,800 depending on season. Savelanded maintains the exact same transparent base pricing structure as leading market providers like Dechaser, competing through superior operational transparency, customer care, and cargo safety.

2. How much does it cost to ship 1kg from China to Nigeria?

For small parcel air cargo under 10kg, rates typically range between $11.50 and $13.50 per kg for general goods, and $13.50 to $15.50 per kg for sensitive items containing batteries. Shipments are subject to a minimum parcel handling charge. Destination clearing in Lagos is either bundled or assessed at approximately ₦1,200 per kg.

3. How long does air freight take?

Standard air freight from Guangzhou or Shenzhen to Lagos (Murtala Muhammed International Airport) typically takes 5 to 8 working days from flight departure. Express courier options take approximately 3 to 5 business days for urgent commercial samples and documents.

4. How long does sea freight take?

Ocean freight sailing time from major Chinese ports (Ningbo-Zhoushan, Guangzhou Nansha, Shenzhen Shekou, Shanghai, Qingdao) to Lagos ports (Apapa, Tin Can Island, Lekki Deep Sea Port) takes approximately 35 to 45 sailing days. Terminal discharge and customs clearance typically require an additional 5 to 10 working days.

5. Is air freight cheaper than sea freight?

No. For commercial volume and heavy goods, sea freight is drastically more cost-effective than air freight—frequently saving importers 70% to 85% per unit. Air freight is only cheaper in total dollar spend for very small, lightweight packages (under 15–20kg) where paying for the minimum ocean CBM space would not be economical.

6. What is the minimum shipping weight?

For air cargo, Savelanded accepts commercial shipments starting from 1kg (billed at the minimum parcel tier threshold). For ocean freight LCL (groupage), our minimum accepted volume starts from as low as 0.1 CBM, allowing growing importers to access economical sea shipping without waiting to fill a full container.

7. What is CBM?

CBM stands for Cubic Meter (m³). It is the standard international metric unit used in ocean freight to measure cargo volume. One CBM equals a cube that measures 1 meter long, 1 meter wide, and 1 meter high (100cm × 100cm × 100cm = 1,000,000 cubic centimeters).

8. How do I calculate shipping volume?

To calculate volume in CBM: multiply carton Length (cm) × Width (cm) × Height (cm), then divide by 1,000,000, and multiply by the total number of cartons. For example: 10 cartons measuring 60cm × 40cm × 50cm = (60 × 40 × 50 ÷ 1,000,000) × 10 = 1.20 CBM. You can use our on-page interactive CBM calculator for instant calculations.

16. What documents do I need to import goods?

For formal full container imports (FCL), Nigeria Customs Service requires an e-Form M, Pre-Arrival Assessment Report (PAAR), Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and a SONCAP Certificate (or NAFDAC permit for food/cosmetics/chemicals). However, for consolidated DDP groupage shipments with Savelanded, we clear your goods under our corporate trade master manifest, eliminating individual documentation complexity for you.

17. Are customs duties included in shipping prices?

Under our Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) shipping service, customs duties, port handling charges, and terminal clearing fees are 100% included in the quoted per-kg or per-CBM price. For port-to-port ocean container shipping (FCL), statutory government customs duties and terminal dues are assessed separately based on your PAAR valuation.
