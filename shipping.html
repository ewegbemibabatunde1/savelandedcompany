How to Calculate China to Nigeria Shipping Weights & Volumes

Ocean shipping is billed per Cubic Meter (CBM): Formula is [Length (cm) × Width (cm) × Height (cm)] ÷ 1,000,000 × number of cartons. Air freight is billed per kilogram based on the higher of physical scale weight vs dimensional volumetric weight: Formula is [Length (cm) × Width (cm) × Height (cm)] ÷ 6,000 per carton. Practical Freight Cost Calculation Walkthrough

Let us walk through a practical scenario: A Lagos boutique owner orders 10 cartons of fashion apparel from a supplier in Guangzhou.

Consignment Details:
• Number of Cartons: 10
• Dimensions per carton: 60cm × 50cm × 40cm
• Scale weight per carton: 15 kg (Total gross weight = 150 kg)

Calculation 1: Air Freight Option
Total volume = 10 × (60 × 50 × 40) = 1,200,000 cm³.
Air Volumetric Weight = 1,200,000 ÷ 6,000 = 200 kg.
Because the volumetric weight (200 kg) is higher than the scale weight (150 kg), airlines will bill on 200 kg chargeable weight.

Calculation 2: Sea Freight LCL Option
Total volume in CBM = 10 × (0.60m × 0.50m × 0.40m) = 1.20 CBM.
Billed strictly on 1.2 CBM ocean freight.

Conclusion: If the merchant needs goods in 1 week for a festive launch, Air Freight (200 kg) is selected. If planning inventory for the next quarter, Sea Freight (1.2 CBM) costs approximately 75% less.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions about our operations and services.

How do I calculate CBM (Cubic Meters) for sea shipping?

To calculate CBM: multiply carton Length (cm) × Width (cm) × Height (cm), then divide by 1,000,000. Multiply by total cartons. For example: 10 cartons measuring 60 × 40 × 50 cm = 1.20 CBM. You can use our on-page interactive CBM calculator.

How do I calculate volumetric weight for air freight to Lagos?

For air cargo: multiply Length (cm) × Width (cm) × Height (cm) and divide by 6,000. If the resulting volumetric weight exceeds the physical scale weight, airlines bill at the volumetric weight.

Should I choose air freight or sea freight for my shipment?

Choose air freight (5–8 days) for time-sensitive, lightweight, or high-value items (electronics, clothing, urgent samples). Choose sea freight (28–38 days) for heavy industrial machinery, construction materials, or high-volume wholesale inventory to minimize cost per unit.
