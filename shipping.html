Air Freight vs. Sea Freight: Making the Right Choice

Selecting between air cargo and sea cargo depends primarily on your cargo's total weight/volume, urgency, and profit margins. Review our quick comparison below:

Factor Air Freight Sea Freight (LCL / FCL)
Primary Advantage Maximum speed, lower holding inventory Lowest cost per unit on bulk cargo
Billing Unit Per Kilogram (kg) Per Cubic Metre (CBM) or per Container
Typical Cargo Phones, smart watches, fashion runs, samples Heavy machinery, furniture, building materials
Transit Timeline Fast scheduled flight routing to Lagos Ocean maritime transit to Lagos ports
Weight Limit Higher cost for heavy/bulky goods Ideal for multi-tonne commercial loads
