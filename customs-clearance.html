Overview

Expert Customs Brokerage for Seamless Port Clearance

Clearing commercial cargo in Nigeria requires meticulous documentation, precise Harmonized System (HS) code classification, and in-depth knowledge of Nigeria Customs Service (NCS) operating procedures. Even a minor discrepancy on a commercial invoice or packing list can trigger demurrage penalties and lengthy cargo detentions. Up-to-date procedures are governed by the Nigeria Customs Service (NCS).

Savelanded provides fully compliant customs brokerage. We coordinate tariff assessments, statutory duty payments, terminal inspections, and security gate passes to ensure your goods move rapidly from quay apron to your warehouse. If you require final delivery across Nigeria, check our integrated door-to-door logistics network.

Official Nigerian Import Regulations: Form M, PAAR & SONCAP

Clearing commercial cargo smoothly through Nigerian ports requires strict adherence to regulations established by the Nigeria Customs Service (NCS), the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), and the Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON).

Whether importing full containers (FCL) requiring individual statutory documentation or utilizing our consolidated groupage clearance, understanding these instruments protects your trade legality.

Mandatory for commercial foreign exchange allocation and PAAR generation. Commercial FCL container sea shipments and high-value formal air cargo.

Harmonized System (HS) Codes & Duty Calculation Structure

Every product entering Nigeria must be accurately classified under the ECOWAS Common External Tariff (CET) using 10-digit Harmonized System (HS) codes. Misclassifying an HS Code to evade duty is considered false declaration under the Nigeria Customs Service Act and carries severe penalties, seizure, and demurrage.

Standard Nigeria Customs Duty Structure (CET)

Total Payable Duty = Import Duty (0%, 5%, 10%, or 20% of CIF value) + 7.5% VAT + 7% Port Surcharge + 0.5% Comprehensive Import Supervision Scheme (CISS) + 0.5% ECOWAS Trade Liberalization Scheme (ETLS).
Savelanded assists clients with pre-classification audits to ensure full compliance while legitimately optimizing duty liability.

Critical Customs Mistakes That Lead to Port Demurrage

  • ×
    Discrepancies Between Invoice & Physical Cargo: If the packing list states 500 cartons of hardware but the physical container holds 450 cartons of hardware and 50 cartons of unlisted electronics, the container is immediately red-flagged for 100% manual examination.
  • ×
    Delayed Form M and PAAR Processing: Initiating Form M after the ship has already departed China leads to cargo arriving at Lagos ports before PAAR is issued, racking up exorbitant terminal demurrage and shipping line detention charges.
  • ×
    Importing Items on the Absolute Import Prohibition List: Nigeria maintains strict prohibitions on certain commodities to encourage local production (e.g., certain bagged mineral waters, second-hand clothing, select pharmaceutical categories). Check with Savelanded before committing capital.

Frequently Asked Questions

What ports does Savelanded clear commercial cargo through in Nigeria?

Savelanded clears cargo through all major maritime and air terminals in Lagos: Apapa Port (APM Terminals, Lilypond), Tin Can Island Port (TICT, Port & Cargo), and Murtala Muhammed International Airport (NAHCO and SAHCO cargo sheds).
