Overview

Expert Customs Brokerage for Seamless Port Clearance

Clearing commercial cargo in Nigeria requires meticulous documentation, precise Harmonized System (HS) code classification, and in-depth knowledge of Nigeria Customs Service (NCS) operating procedures. Even a minor discrepancy on a commercial invoice or packing list can trigger demurrage penalties and lengthy cargo detentions. Up-to-date procedures are governed by the Nigeria Customs Service (NCS).

Savelanded provides fully compliant customs brokerage. We coordinate tariff assessments, statutory duty payments, terminal inspections, and security gate passes to ensure your goods move rapidly from quay apron to your warehouse. If you require final delivery across Nigeria, check our integrated door-to-door logistics network.

Essential Import Documentation We Manage

  • ✓
    Form M Processing: Mandatory documentation registered with authorized dealer banks and approved by the Central Bank of Nigeria.
  • ✓
    Pre-Arrival Assessment Report (PAAR): Official duty valuation document issued by NCS headquarters to confirm tariff classification.
  • ✓
    Regulatory Clearances: SONCAP conformity certifications through the Standards Organisation of Nigeria, NAFDAC permits, and quarantine approvals.
  • ✓
    Accurate HS Code Assignment: Applying the correct tariff headings prevents overpayment of duties and administrative penalty surcharges.

Our 4-Step Terminal Release Workflow

  • 1
    Pre-Arrival Audit: We audit all shipping documents 7 to 10 days before vessel arrival to eliminate paperwork errors.
  • 2
    Duty Assessment & Payment: We generate the Single Goods Declaration (SGD) and facilitate direct CBN-approved duty payment.
  • 3
    Physical / Scanning Examination: Our licensed clearance officers attend physical joint examinations with customs inspection officers.
  • 4
    Terminal Exit & Trucking: Terminal delivery order (TDO) release, gate pass issuance, and bonded truck dispatch to your address.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions about our operations and services.

What ports does Savelanded clear commercial cargo through in Nigeria?

Savelanded clears cargo through all major maritime and air terminals in Lagos: Apapa Port (APM Terminals, Lilypond), Tin Can Island Port (TICT, Port & Cargo), and Murtala Muhammed International Airport (NAHCO and SAHCO cargo sheds).

Does Savelanded manage Form M and PAAR documentation?

Yes. For commercial consignees, we process Form M bank registration, Pre-Arrival Assessment Report (PAAR) issuance with the Nigeria Customs Service, duty valuation, and SONCAP/NAFDAC regulatory certifications.

Can Savelanded clear goods under DDP without the client having an import license?

Yes. For consolidated air cargo and LCL sea freight groupage, Savelanded provides all-inclusive Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) clearing under our corporate license, requiring zero paperwork from the client.
