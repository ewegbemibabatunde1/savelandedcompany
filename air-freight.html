How Air Freight Billing Works: Volumetric vs. Actual Weight

In international air transport, cargo space is limited. Airlines bill based on whichever is higher: the physical gross weight (kg) or the volumetric (dimensional) weight.

Standard Air Freight Volumetric Formula:
Volumetric Weight (kg) = [Length (cm) × Width (cm) × Height (cm)] ÷ 6,000
Example: A carton measuring 60cm × 50cm × 40cm has a volume of 120,000 cm³. Divided by 6,000, its volumetric weight is 20 kg. If the scale weight is only 12 kg, billing is charged at 20 kg.

Chargeable Weight Calculation Examples: Actual vs. Volumetric Weight

One of the most frequent sources of confusion for Nigerian importers is how airlines calculate air cargo costs. Following IATA (International Air Transport Association) rules, air freight charges are always billed based on whichever is greater: the Actual Gross Scale Weight or the Volumetric (Dimensional) Weight.

IATA Air Cargo Volumetric Formula

Volumetric Weight (kg) = [Length (cm) × Width (cm) × Height (cm)] ÷ 6,000
Note: Some express courier services like DHL or FedEx use a 5,000 divisor, making commercial air cargo with a 6,000 divisor significantly more economical for bulky goods.

Cargo Scenario Carton Dimensions Actual Weight Volumetric Weight Billed Weight & Explanation
Heavy Dense Goods (e.g., copper wire, hardware tools) 40cm × 30cm × 25cm (30,000 cm³) 22.0 kg 30,000 ÷ 6,000 = 5.0 kg 22.0 kg (Actual scale weight applies because physical mass exceeds dimensional volume)
Bulky Lightweight Goods (e.g., shoes in boxes, hats, plush toys) 70cm × 60cm × 50cm (210,000 cm³) 14.0 kg 210,000 ÷ 6,000 = 35.0 kg 35.0 kg (Volumetric weight applies because cargo consumes significant aircraft hold space)
Balanced Electronics Carton (e.g., smart watches, phone accessories) 50cm × 40cm × 30cm (60,000 cm³) 11.5 kg 60,000 ÷ 6,000 = 10.0 kg 11.5 kg (Scale weight slightly exceeds volume; billed on exact 11.5 kg)

Air Freight Restrictions & Dangerous Goods (DG) Guidelines

International aviation safety regulations strictly enforce what can and cannot board cargo aircraft. Understanding these restrictions saves Nigerian businesses weeks of delays and prevents cargo confiscation at airport customs.

Standard Air Clearance

General Cargo (General Goods)

Apparel, unbranded footwear, machine spare parts, phone cases, solar lamps (without internal lithium batteries), and hardware accessories. Clears customs rapidly with standard commercial invoice and packing list.

Special DG Routing

Battery & Electronic Goods

Phones, laptops, power banks, and rechargeable tools require Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS), UN38.3 test summaries, and dangerous goods declarations under ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization) rules. Flown via designated battery freighter lanes.

Strictly Prohibited Air Items

Restricted Substances

Flammable liquids, perfumes, aerosols, uncertified powders, corrosive acids, perishable fresh foods without reefer air containers, and brand-infringing (counterfeit) designer items are strictly rejected by airline security.

Required Documentation & Route Logistics

Every air cargo consignment moving between China and Lagos requires rigorous cross-border documentation:

  • •
    Air Waybill (AWB): The fundamental contract of carriage issued by the carrier or forwarder detailing shipper, consignee, airport codes (CAN/SZX to LOS), weight, and piece count.
  • •
    Commercial Invoice (CI) & Packing List (PL): Itemized list of merchandise, unit values, gross/net weights, and harmonized system (HS) codes.
  • •
    Lagos Airport Handling Sheds: Upon landing at Murtala Muhammed International Airport (LOS), cargo is received into official cargo handling sheds operated by NAHCO (Nigerian Aviation Handling Company) or SAHCO (Skyway Aviation Handling Company) before Nigeria Customs Service inspection.

Air Freight Cost Drivers & Transit Expectations

Air cargo rates fluctuate dynamically based on aviation fuel surcharges (baf), airline peak season demand (especially from September to December before Christmas and Chinese New Year), and cargo classification (General cargo vs. DG battery rates).

Standard air transit: 5 to 8 working days from flight departure in Guangzhou. Priority express: 3 to 5 working days. Note that supplier production delays or Chinese domestic courier delays to our warehouse are outside the flight transit window.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions about our operations and services.

How long does air freight take from China to Nigeria with Savelanded?

Standard scheduled air cargo takes approximately 5 to 8 working days from departure at Guangzhou Baiyun (CAN) to Lagos MMIA Airport customs release. Priority express cargo delivers in 3 to 5 working days.

How is volumetric weight calculated for air cargo to Lagos?

Following IATA (International Air Transport Association) rules, volumetric weight in kilograms is calculated as (Length × Width × Height in cm) ÷ 6,000. Airlines charge the higher number between the actual scale weight and the volumetric weight.

Can I ship phones, electronics, or battery products by air?

Yes. Commercial consumer electronics, accessories, and machinery parts ship on regular scheduled flights. Lithium battery-bearing electronics require MSDS compliance documents and fly via specialized dangerous goods (DG) routes.
