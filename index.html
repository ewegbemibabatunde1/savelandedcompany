Freight Solutions

Choose the Shipping Solution That Works for You

Align your transport mode with your inventory timeline, cargo type, and commercial objectives.

Air Freight
Priority Speed • Fast Turnaround

Appropriate for customers prioritising speed and suitable time-sensitive cargo.

  • Best For: High-value electronics, samples, urgent seasonal inventory, and critical spare parts.
  • Operations: Regular scheduled flight departures from Guangzhou and international airport terminals.
  • Conditions: Subject to standard airline cargo restrictions and applicable air transport charges.
LEARN ABOUT AIR FREIGHT
Sea Freight
Maximum Economy • Bulk Volume

Appropriate for larger shipments and customers prioritising cargo volume and shipping economics.

  • Best For: Heavy industrial machinery, wholesale merchandise, furniture, and high CBM volume.
  • Container Options: Full Container Load (FCL 20ft/40ft) and Less than Container Load (LCL groupage).
  • Economics: Lowest cost per cubic metre/ton, directly protecting wholesale profit margins.
