Protection & Discovery

Direct Factory Access Without the Online Scams

Many Nigerian merchants attempt to source goods online through platforms like 1688, Taobao, or social media channels, only to encounter trading intermediaries acting as factories, substandard product substitutions, or outright fraudulent accounts.

With Savelanded's registered presence in Yiwu City (Zhejiang Province) and Guangzhou (Guangdong Province), you gain a trusted physical team that walks the factory floors, inspects production machinery, reviews raw material quality, and negotiates genuine wholesale factory pricing on your behalf.

Our 5-Point Supplier Verification Standard

  • 1
    Corporate Registry Search: Verification of official unified social credit code, capital base, legal representative, and litigation history via Chinese registry databases.
  • 2
    Physical Factory vs. Trader Audit: Confirming whether the vendor owns the factory premises or is merely an unlicensed middleman taking margin cuts.
  • 3
    Production Line & Capacity Check: Evaluating daily manufacturing output to verify whether the supplier can meet your delivery timeline.
  • 4
    Sample Quality Evaluation: Procuring first-off production samples, examining stitching, weight, electronic functionality, and durability.
  • 5
    Legally Binding Chinese Purchase Agreement: Structuring formal purchase contracts specifying defect thresholds, packing standards, and delivery terms.

What You Receive in a Savelanded Sourcing Report

  • Full supplier corporate credentials and contact details

  • High-resolution photos and video walkthroughs of the product
