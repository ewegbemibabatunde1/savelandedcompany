Overview

Direct Access to Authentic Chinese Manufacturers

Nigeria's retail and wholesale markets thrive on fast-moving consumer goods, industrial equipment, construction materials, and electronics sourced from China. However, searching online from Lagos often exposes buyers to trading middlemen, inflated prices, and scam accounts. Quality standards can be benchmarked against the Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON).

With permanent bilingual operations in Yiwu and Guangzhou, Savelanded directly bridges this gap. We bypass middlemen, taking your product requirements directly to the actual manufacturing workshops and wholesale cluster markets. Once verified, we facilitate seamless currency settlement through our procurement and RMB purchasing service.

Our 5-Point Factory Audit & Verification Protocol

  • 1
    Legal Registration Verification: We examine business licenses, registered operating capital, and SAMR corporate records in China.
  • 2
    Physical Workshop or Stall Visit: Our team visits the facility to confirm real manufacturing capability rather than an unlicensed shell broker.
  • 3
    Production Line & Capacity Check: We verify daily output, machine condition, and workforce availability to guarantee project delivery timelines.
  • 4
    Bilingual Price Negotiation: We negotiate strictly in Mandarin at domestic wholesale price points, securing volume rebates for you.
  • 5
    Comprehensive Sourcing Dossier: We deliver a complete report with factory photos, quotation sheets, lead times, and unit carton specs.

Sample Acquisition & Pre-Production Testing

Before committing your capital to a full production run or container order, Savelanded arranges physical sample purchasing. We inspect the sample at our Yiwu office, test materials and functionality, film detailed 4K review videos for your approval, or dispatch the sample to your Nigerian office via express courier.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions about our operations and services.

How does Savelanded verify if a Chinese supplier or factory is real?

We execute a strict 5-stage factory audit: checking registration numbers on the Chinese SAMR database, physically inspecting their stall in Yiwu International Trade City or manufacturing floor in Guangdong, testing live machinery capacity, obtaining pre-production samples, and confirming legitimate domestic RMB bank accounts.

Can Savelanded help me purchase wholesale goods from 1688.com or Taobao?

Yes. Savelanded acts as your direct purchasing and communication agent on 1688.com. We communicate with factory representatives in Mandarin, negotiate domestic wholesale discounts, disburse payments in Chinese Yuan (RMB) or Alipay, receive parcels at our Yiwu/Guangzhou hubs, and inspect quality.

Can I get private labeling (OEM) and custom packaging in China?

Yes. Savelanded coordinates OEM and ODM manufacturing, including custom mold fabrication, logo printing, barcode labeling, user manual translations, and branded retail packaging.
