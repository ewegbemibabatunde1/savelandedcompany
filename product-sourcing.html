Supplier Discovery & On-Site Due Diligence

Online marketplaces like 1688.com, Made-in-China, and Alibaba list millions of vendors, but up to 40% are trading middlemen or shell entities rather than direct manufacturers. Savelanded performs multi-point on-site verifications across China:

1

Business License Audit

Verification of the supplier's Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) against the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) database to confirm authorized business scope, registered capital, and operational status.

2

Factory Floor Inspection

Physical visit to the production workshop to confirm machinery presence, worker headcount, daily output capacity, and compliance with quality control standards.

3

Sample Testing & Approval

Receiving pre-production samples at our Yiwu or Guangzhou office, examining material thickness, seam strength, technical specs, and couriering directly to you in Nigeria for sign-off.

Common China Sourcing Mistakes & How to Prevent Them

Nigerian entrepreneurs frequently lose capital to avoidable purchasing blunders. Savelanded acts as your protective ground buffer:

  • ×
    Ordering Without Pre-Shipment Sample Approval: Never initiate full production based solely on catalog photos. Subtle changes in fabric weight (GSM) or plastic grade can ruin marketability.
  • ×
    Paying 100% Upfront: Standard international terms are 30% deposit to begin production, and 70% balance payable only AFTER post-production quality inspection passes.
  • ×
    Vague Purchase Specifications: "Good quality" has no legal standing in China. Specifications must document exact Pantone colors, dimensions, voltage (220V for Nigeria), plug types (UK standard Type G), and carton burst-strength.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions about our operations and services.

How does Savelanded verify if a Chinese supplier or factory is real?

We execute a strict 5-stage factory audit: checking registration numbers on the Chinese SAMR database, physically inspecting their stall in Yiwu International Trade City or manufacturing floor in Guangdong, testing live machinery capacity, obtaining pre-production samples, and confirming legitimate domestic RMB bank accounts.

Can Savelanded help me purchase wholesale goods from 1688.com or Taobao?

Yes. Savelanded acts as your direct purchasing and communication agent on 1688.com. We communicate with factory representatives in Mandarin, negotiate domestic wholesale discounts, disburse payments in Chinese Yuan (RMB) or Alipay, receive parcels at our Yiwu/Guangzhou hubs, and inspect quality.

Can I get private labeling (OEM) and custom packaging in China?

Yes. Savelanded coordinates OEM and ODM manufacturing, including custom mold fabrication, logo printing, barcode labeling, user manual translations, and branded retail packaging.
