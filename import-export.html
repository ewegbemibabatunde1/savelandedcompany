Overview

Empowering Bilateral Commerce Between China and Nigeria

International trade between China and Nigeria involves multifaceted legal frameworks, currency regulations, and customs inspection protocols on both continents. Misunderstandings regarding export tax documentation in China or import valuation at Nigerian ports can lead to major financial losses. Commercial terms adhere to official guidelines from the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC).

Savelanded provides comprehensive cross-border trade structuring. Whether you are establishing an ongoing supply chain for industrial equipment or requiring export customs clearance in Ningbo and Shenzhen, our bilingual specialists protect your transactions from end to end. We connect seamlessly with our customs documentation services and ocean container operations.

Understanding Incoterms: Choosing the Right Contract Structure

Incoterm Seller / Factory Responsibility Buyer / Savelanded Responsibility Risk Transfer Point
EXW (Ex Works) Makes goods available at Chinese factory gate Pick up, domestic Chinese trucking, export clearance, ocean freight, Nigerian clearance Factory gate in China
FOB (Free on Board) Domestic transport + China export customs + loading onto vessel Ocean freight shipping, marine insurance, Nigerian customs, inland delivery Ship's rail at Chinese port
CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight) Ocean freight and basic maritime cargo insurance to Lagos port Port terminal discharge, customs clearance, duty payment, local delivery Lagos port terminal
DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) Complete door-to-door delivery with all export and import duties paid Collect cargo directly at your Lagos warehouse Buyer's doorstep

Understanding Total Landed Cost: The Importer's Profitability Formula

A common failure among new Nigerian importers is calculating profit margins based solely on the supplier's ex-factory quotation. True business profitability depends on calculating your Total Landed Cost per unit.

Cost Element Description How to Calculate & Control
1. Ex-Factory Price (EXW) The unit manufacturing price charged by the Chinese factory. Negotiate tiered volume discounts or purchase on 1688 via Savelanded to avoid middleman markups.
2. China Domestic Logistics Courier or truck transportation from factory to our Guangzhou/Yiwu warehouse. Consolidate orders from multiple vendors at one hub to eliminate redundant courier drops.
3. International Freight (Air / Sea) Cross-border carriage from China airport or seaport to Lagos. Select Sea Freight (LCL/FCL) for high volume and non-urgent inventory to minimize cost per kg/CBM.
4. Terminal Handling & Customs Duty Port levies, NCS import duty, PAAR/Form M statutory charges, and agency clearing. Ensure accurate HS classification; choose Savelanded all-inclusive groupage to eliminate hidden port fees.
5. Nigerian Last-Mile Dispatch Trucking from Lagos port or Trade Fair depot to your retail warehouse or retail shop. Coordinate direct interstate haulage via Savelanded logistics network across all 36 states.

Commercial Incoterms 2020: EXW, FOB & CIF Explained

Published by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC), Incoterms define the exact point where risk and freight cost transfer from seller to buyer:

Best with Savelanded

EXW (Ex-Works)

The seller makes goods available at their factory floor. Savelanded handles domestic collection in China, warehouse receiving, export customs, and onward transit. Offers maximum cost control.

Common for Sea FCL

FOB (Free on Board)

The Chinese factory pays domestic transport and export port clearance, delivering goods loaded on the vessel at Ningbo or Guangzhou. Savelanded manages ocean freight and Lagos clearing.

Higher Hidden Costs

CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight)

The seller arranges ocean freight to Lagos port. While convenient, foreign forwarders often impose exorbitant destination terminal handling fees upon arrival in Nigeria.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions about our operations and services.

What are Incoterms and which is best for importing from China to Nigeria?

Incoterms (International Commercial Terms) are international shipping trade rules published by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC). For most Nigerian businesses, DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) or CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight) is recommended, as Savelanded takes charge of carriage, transit insurance, and customs clearance.

Can Savelanded help with export clearance from China?

Yes. We coordinate Chinese customs export declarations with the General Administration of Customs of China (GACC), Certificate of Origin (Form B) issuance, fumigation compliance, and export VAT refunds.

What agricultural products can be exported from Nigeria to China?

Savelanded facilitates bilateral export logistics for raw cashew nuts, sesame seeds, dried split ginger, cassava chips, and solid minerals meeting Chinese import quarantine standards.
