Overview

The Ultimate Hassle-Free Shipping Solution

Managing multiple independent service providers—such as a Chinese warehouse agent, an airline, a maritime carrier, a Lagos clearing agent, and a local trucking firm—is stressful and full of finger-pointing when delays or cargo damage occur. Domestic distribution networks are operated in alignment with the Nigerian Ports Authority (NPA) operational guidelines.

Savelanded's Door-to-Door Cargo Delivery Service unifies the entire supply chain under a single point of accountability. One invoice, one tracking number, and zero hidden clearance surprises upon cargo arrival. Need help verifying products before shipping? Review our China product sourcing and inspection service.

Why Nigerian Importers Prefer Our Door-to-Door Service

All-Inclusive (DDP) Pricing
Your rate covers everything: freight, customs assessment, terminal handling, and port duties. You never pay unexpected terminal demurrage.
Zero Customs Bureaucracy
No need to register complex Form M paperwork or negotiate with port inspection officers. Savelanded handles the entire clearance process.
Nationwide Delivery Coverage
We deliver to major commercial commercial centers across all 36 states, including Lagos, Abuja, Onitsha, Kano, Port Harcourt, and Ibadan.
Cargo Insurance & Security
Every carton is tracked with unique barcode identifiers from intake in Guangzhou or Yiwu until final handover at your doorstep.

Lagos Trade Fair Depot & Interstate Distribution

Our Nigerian headquarters and primary sorting warehouse is situated at Zone B, Shop 13/14, 23/24, Edo Plaza, Balogun, Trade Fair Complex, Badagry Expressway, Ojo, Lagos.

For clients within Lagos, you can collect directly from our warehouse or request delivery by our fleet of distribution vans. For interstate clients, cargo is securely palletized and transferred to verified interstate logistics lines with waybill tracking provided immediately.

The End-to-End Cargo Journey

  • 1
    Intake in China: Factory delivers goods to our Yiwu or Guangzhou warehouse.
  • 2
    Packaging & Departure: We consolidate, waterproof, crate, and load goods onto our scheduled flight or ocean container.
  • 3
    Customs Clearance: Our licensed Lagos customs team clears the consignment at the port without client involvement.
  • 4
    Delivery & Handover: Your cargo arrives safely at your doorstep or our Trade Fair distribution hub for easy pickup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions about our operations and services.

What is included in Savelanded's door-to-door freight rate?

Our door-to-door rate is 100% all-inclusive (DDP). It covers China domestic warehouse intake, international air or ocean freight, Lagos port terminal fees, Nigerian customs clearance, import duties, and final delivery to your store or warehouse.

Where in Nigeria can Savelanded deliver my goods?

From our Lagos sorting center at Trade Fair Complex, we deliver throughout Lagos state and dispatch via verified interstate logistics networks across all 36 Nigerian states including Abuja, Onitsha, Kano, Port Harcourt, Aba, and Ibadan.

How do I track my door-to-door cargo?

Every parcel is assigned a unique tracking mark upon intake in Yiwu or Guangzhou. Clients receive real-time departure manifests, port clearing updates, and delivery confirmations via WhatsApp and email.
