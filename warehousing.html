Overview

Maximize Space, Minimize Shipping Costs

When purchasing from several suppliers across China—such as bags from Guangzhou, small electronics from Shenzhen, and accessories from Yiwu—shipping each batch separately creates redundant documentation and excessive handling fees. Warehousing operations adhere to global packaging benchmarks from the World Customs Organization (WCO).

Savelanded's Warehousing and Consolidation Service solves this. Suppliers dispatch your orders to our receiving depots. We receive every package, log it against your client marking code, inspect carton integrity, and consolidate everything into one unified consignment for air shipping or sea container transport.

Guangzhou Consolidation Warehouse

How Multi-Supplier Cargo Consolidation Works

Most successful Nigerian merchants do not buy all their merchandise from a single factory. You might purchase shoes in Wenzhou, bags in Yiwu, and phone chargers in Shenzhen. Paying separate shipping and clearing fees for each individual package is financially prohibitive.

Our multi-supplier cargo consolidation unites all your purchases into a single unified consignment, cutting your landed shipping costs by up to 35%.

1

Intake & Unique Shipping Mark

Suppliers deliver to our Guangzhou or Yiwu warehouse marked with your unique client code (e.g., SLC-LAGOS-XXXX). Every carton is scanned into our tracking registry.

2

Inspection, Weighing & CBM Measurement

We inspect outer cartons for transit damage, record scale weight, measure precise dimensional length × width × height, and photograph goods for your verification.

3

Free Storage & Repacking

Goods are safely stored in our secure, dry warehouse while awaiting other supplier deliveries. We provide complimentary storage up to 14 days to complete your collection.

4

Reinforced Packaging & Dispatch

Cartons are grouped, consolidated, reinforced with high-density strapping, waterproof woven sacks, and corner protectors before loading into air pallets or sea containers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions about our operations and services.

How does cargo consolidation reduce shipping fees from China?

Consolidation merges multiple packages from different Chinese suppliers into fewer master cartons, eliminating redundant packaging and dead space. This significantly lowers both CBM for sea freight and volumetric weight for air cargo, while eliminating multiple separate clearing charges in Lagos.

How many days of free warehouse storage does Savelanded provide in China?

We offer up to 30 days of 100% free storage at both our Yiwu Head Office (16, 2nd St, Longgang Rd) and our Guangzhou warehouse (Huayag Warehouse, No. 498 Qinglong Rd), giving you ample time to collect shipments from multiple factories.

What protective packaging does Savelanded offer for fragile goods?

We provide 5-ply export carton repacking, heavy-duty waterproof shrink wrap, impact corner guards, and custom fumigated wooden crating for machinery, electronics, ceramics, and solar equipment.
